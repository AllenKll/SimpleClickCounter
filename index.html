Simple Tap Counter
Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Simple Tap Counter is an offline counting app. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your current count and one previous count in private app storage on your device. These values let you continue counting after closing the app and undo your most recent action. They are not sent to Screaming Goblin or any third party.

No tracking or online services

The app has no advertisements, analytics, accounts, or third-party tracking libraries. It does not request internet access or access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Cloud backup and device transfer of saved counts are disabled.

Storage and deletion

Saved counts remain on your device until replaced by later actions, cleared through Android Settings, or deleted when you uninstall the app. Reset sets the current count to zero while keeping the previous count available for one-step Undo. To remove all saved values, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it.

Security

Saved counts use Android's private app storage. We do not operate a server that receives your counts or personal data from this app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
